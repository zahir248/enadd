Our services

End-to-end logistics capability

From air and sea freight to heavy lift project cargo and ship’s spares, we coordinate the details so you can focus on your business.

Local & regional transport

Local cargo pick-up, Malaysia–Singapore trucking, and coordination for onward delivery.

Air & sea freight

Air and sea freight forwarding, negotiation of special spot rates for bulk cargo with airlines and carriers.

FTZ & customs

Import clearance from various FTZs in Singapore, delivery to local address, and FTZ warehouse operations.

Cargo handling & value-add

Fumigation, packing and repacking, inward / outward / transhipment cargo handling.

Project & heavy lift

Specialised heavy lift, project cargo, and odd-size shipments with careful planning and execution.

Marine & shipping support

Ship’s spare in transit and worldwide onboard delivery, shipping agencies support, OPL boat, lighterage, tug & barge supply.

Insurance & express

Marine insurance arrangements and worldwide hand-carry service for time-critical movements.
